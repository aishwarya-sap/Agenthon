The model is invoked at **two bounded touch-points only** (see L0-8.2 classification): (a) intent framing and (b) one-line verdict synthesis. All quantitative reasoning — consequence simulation, decision matrix, HITL checkpoints — is **deterministic Python**, not model-driven.

## 2. Why a frontier model is required at these two steps
- **Verdict synthesis** must produce a defensible, audience-correct natural-language summary of a multi-signal procurement scorecard for a Head of Department. Smaller models tested in the interim suite produced verdicts that mislabelled the recommended action or dropped the mandatory financial disclaimer.
- **Intent framing** must robustly map free-text, multi-language HoD queries (see eval dataset INTENT/multilingual records) onto the correct analysis path. This is the step where a weaker model most often misroutes.
- Because the deterministic core already bounds cost and removes reasoning from the quantitative path, the frontier model is used **narrowly** — the justification is for quality on language tasks, not for autonomy.
