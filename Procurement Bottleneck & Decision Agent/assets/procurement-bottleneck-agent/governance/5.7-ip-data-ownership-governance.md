## 1. Output ownership
- AI-generated outputs (scorecards, recommendations, verdict text) are **work product delivered to the customer (Adarsh Developers)** for their internal procurement decisioning. Final ownership follows the governing SAP engagement/customer agreement — **confirm with Legal on tenant**.

## 2. Customer data isolation
- Tenant isolation at the SAP BTP subaccount boundary; access enforced by XSUAA OAuth2 + principal propagation (`app/agent_executor.py`, `xs-security.json`).
- Institutional memory (decision log) and any RAG corpus are **per-tenant**; no cross-tenant read.

## 3. Non-use in training
- **Customer data is not used in any model training or fine-tuning step.** The agent consumes a hosted model via Generative AI Hub and performs no training (see `2.7-model-update-retraining-governance.md`). Attested.

## 4. Copyright position for AI-generated content
- Outputs are factual, data-grounded analyses (figures, rankings) rather than creative works. Copyright position on such outputs follows SAP standard AI terms — **confirm on tenant**.

## 5. Third-party IP / indemnification
- SAP's indemnification position for AI output infringing third-party IP follows the standard SAP Business AI terms — **reference the governing agreement; do not restate from memory**.

## 6. Dispute escalation
- Customer disputes over output ownership escalate via the engagement's GDH APAC architect group → SAP Legal. Path to be confirmed at L0-7.4 sign-off.
