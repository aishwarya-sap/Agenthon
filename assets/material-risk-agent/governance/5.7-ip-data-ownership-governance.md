# IP Rights & Data Ownership Governance — L0-5.7

**Agent:** material-risk-agent
**Status:** Documented as a distinct artefact. Customer owns all SAP/project data and generated drafts; hosted model, no customer data used for training; no third-party data ingested beyond the customer's own sources.

Checklist item **L0-5.7** requires a standalone IP/ownership record stating output ownership, tenant isolation, non-use of customer data in training, copyright position, and the dispute escalation path.

## 1. Data ownership

The **customer owns all input data**: SAP S/4HANA records (MM-IM, MM-PUR, PS), the Excel project schedule, and the supplier mailbox content indexed into the RAG corpus. SAP/the delivery team claim no ownership over customer business data.

## 2. Output / generated-content ownership

AI-generated drafts — expedite-PO emails, escalation narratives, advisory reports — are **customer IP**. The agent is advisory: it produces drafts and recommendations the customer reviews, approves, and owns. Copyright in generated text vests with the customer.

## 3. Tenant isolation

Customer data is isolated at tenant level: in-tenant HANA Cloud Vector Engine for retrieval and the AI Core resource group in the customer's S/4 region for inference. No pooling across tenants.

## 4. Non-use in training

**No customer data is used in any model training or fine-tuning step.** Models are hosted and consumed read-only via Generative AI Hub; customer data appears only as transient inference-time context. (Consistent with L0-2.7.)

## 5. Third-party data

**No third-party data is ingested** beyond the customer's own SAP + Excel + mailbox sources, all under the customer's existing agreements. The only external signal (`EXTERNAL_RISK_FEED`) is simulated and clearly labelled — it introduces no third-party IP.

## 6. Escalation path for ownership disputes

Any customer dispute over output ownership routes to the delivery lead and SAP Legal / Compliance via the tenant governance record; this artefact is updated whenever the foundational model or grounding approach changes.
