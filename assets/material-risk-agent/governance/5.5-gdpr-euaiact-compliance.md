# GDPR / EU AI Act Compliance — L0-5.5

**Agent:** material-risk-agent
**Status:** Documented. Classification: **limited-risk AI assistant** (advisory-only, human oversight). PII in supplier emails redacted before the model; data residency in-tenant. Reviewed at tenant cross-functional sign-off (7.4).

Checklist item **L0-5.5** requires a record stating the applicable regulatory category and the evidence satisfying each requirement.

## 1. Personal data processed

| Data | Source | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier contact name / email / phone | Supplier email threads in the RAG corpus | **Redacted before the model** (`redact_pii`): email local-part dropped (domain kept), phone masked |
| Vendor / project / material records | SAP S/4HANA (MM-IM, MM-PUR), Excel schedule | Business data, not personal data; no protected attributes |

No special-category data is processed. The agent acts on materials and schedules, not on individuals.

## 2. GDPR posture

- **Data minimisation:** PII redacted at the retrieval boundary before inference; only the minimum business context reaches the model.
- **Purpose limitation:** data used solely for material-availability / supply-risk advice.
- **Residency:** retrieval corpus in **in-tenant HANA Cloud Vector Engine**; inference via the **AI Core resource group in the S/4 region** — no cross-region egress.

## 3. EU AI Act classification

**Limited-risk AI system (transparency obligations).** Rationale: the agent is advisory-only and never autonomously writes SAP business objects; a human makes every consequential decision. It is not a high-risk system under Annex III (no employment, credit, or essential-service decisioning).

| Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|

| Transparency | Responses carry live/simulated labels and a financial disclaimer; AI nature disclosed |
